# Project conventions

## Language
The interface is **multilingual**. Portuguese copy must be **Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR)**,
never European Portuguese. Watch for: gerund over `a + infinitive`
("o mundo rodando", not "o mundo a rodar"); "salvar" not "guardar"; "em uma" not "numa"
in UI labels. English is the secondary line, shown under the PT label.

## Design
Palette and type come from `Cellula v3.dc.html` tokens (Baloo 2 display + Nunito UI,
abyss `#0a1a22`, glow teal `#79dcc4`, amber `#f0a63c`, lime `#a8cf4c`).
Cell/agent art lives in `art/` (imported from harena-lab/dcc) — never hand-draw SVG organisms.
